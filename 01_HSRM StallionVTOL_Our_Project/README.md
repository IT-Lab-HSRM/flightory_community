# 🚁 01 – HSRM StallionVTOL: Our Project

This folder documents our practical course project at the **Technical University Hochschule RheinMain (HSRM)**, where we built a **Flightory Stallion VTOL** system from the ground up.

It serves as a transparent record of our process — including what worked, what didn't, and everything we learned along the way.

Documents in this folder are organized across two practical courses (P1 and P2). 
The prefix in each filename indicates which course it belongs to, making the chronological progression easy to follow.

Our own troubleshooting tips and learnings are of course incoporated in [02_Tips, Learnings & Troubleshooting](/02_Tips,%20Learnings%20%26%20Troubleshooting)

## 📂 What You'll Find Here

- Build process and assembly notes
- Configuration and setup decisions
- Problems we encountered and how we solved them
- Lessons learned and personal takeaways
